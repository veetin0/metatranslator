# Volunteer consent — Signer demo, Junction 2026

**What Signer is.** A student hackathon prototype that turns a few everyday ASL signs into English text on Meta
Ray-Ban Display glasses and speaks it. It is not an interpreter.

**What we ask.** You sign short phrases (from `demo/script.md`) in front of a person wearing the glasses, during
rehearsals and/or the live demo.

**What happens to your data.**
- The glasses camera sees you only after you tap **Start** on the card, and stops when you tap **Stop**.
- Live camera frames are turned into hand and body points in the laptop's memory and dropped. They are never saved
  and never sent to a cloud service.
- No face recognition and no profile of you are made.
- If you use **Teach a sign**, the hand and body points for that sign stay on the team laptop under the sign's name,
  and you can delete them in one tap or ask us to. We delete them after the event.
