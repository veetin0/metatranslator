# Printed table sign (R3) — text

Printed as `demo/consent-sign.pdf` (A4 portrait, from `demo/consent-sign.html` via `bash deck/build.sh`).
Placed on the demo table, facing visitors.

---

**Signer — live ASL demo**

The glasses camera is on only while the signer has tapped **Start**.
When it is on, the card shows: **● Translation ON — camera in use, nothing recorded**.

- Camera frames stay in the laptop's memory and are dropped at once. Nothing is saved or uploaded.
- No face recognition. No profiles.
- Only the signer starts and stops it.

Prefer not to be in view? Tell us and we will stop or turn the camera away.

Everyday phrases only. For medical, legal and exams, book an interpreter.
